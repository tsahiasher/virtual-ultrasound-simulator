| 8 | 2026-08-29 | Antigravity & Tsahi | **Beam-Dependent Cumulative Acoustic Attenuation & Shadow Pipeline ($T(z) = \exp(-2 \int \mu(s) ds)$).**<br>**Why:** Phase 5 replaces simple global depth decay with physical, material-dependent cumulative attenuation integrated along individual acoustic scan lines. Formulating round-trip pulse-echo transmission as $T(z_k) = \exp(-2 \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \mu_j \Delta z \cdot \text{AttenuationScale})$ in physical distance units ($\Delta z$ in meters, $\mu$ in $\text{m}^{-1}$) guarantees sample resolution independence ($\Delta z = z_{\max} / (N_S - 1)$), so changing `SamplesPerScanLine` (e.g. 64 to 1024) preserves exact physical optical depth without darkening or brightening artifacts.
