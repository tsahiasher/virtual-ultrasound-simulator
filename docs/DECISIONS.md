# Architecture Decisions Log

| N | Date | Author | Decision & Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-08-26 | Claude & Tsahi | **CPU Reference Sampling Architecture with Zero Managed Allocation Buffer.**<br>**Why:** Milestone 1 focuses on proving geometric correctness, coordinate transforms, and interactive probe feedback before introducing GPU complexity. Using a CPU generator (`CPUSliceGenerator`) with pre-allocated `SliceBuffer` provides deterministic validation, instantaneous testability, and 60+ FPS performance without shader debugging overhead.<br>**Alternatives Rejected:** Direct compute shader / Texture3D sampling (deferred to Stage 3 to avoid coupling geometric verification with platform-specific compute support).<br>**Proof:** 7/7 unit tests passed in 21ms; 0 per-frame managed heap allocations in slice loop.<br>**Files Affected:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/SliceBuffer.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/CPUSliceGenerator.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/SliceRenderer.cs`. |
| 2 | 2026-08-26 | Claude & Tsahi | **Explicit 4-Tier Coordinate Space Pipeline ($S \to P \to W \to V$).**<br>**Why:** Clear separation between Image UV ($S$), Probe Space ($P$), World Space ($W$), and Volume Space ($V$) prevents hidden coordinate bugs, ad-hoc sign flips, and tightly couples with standard ultrasound geometry.<br>**Alternatives Rejected:** Ad-hoc world space raycasting or baking transforms into UI image space.<br>**Proof:** Forward and inverse round-trip tests pass with precision $< 10^{-4}$ units.<br>**Files Affected:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/CoordinateTransform.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Probe/ProbeGeometry.cs`. |
| 3 | 2026-08-26 | Claude & Tsahi | **Self-Assembling `SceneBootstrapper` for Zero-Setup Unity Execution.**<br>**Why:** Guarantees that opening any empty Unity scene or new project version runs immediately without missing prefab references, broken GUIDs, or manual canvas wiring.<br>**Alternatives Rejected:** Relying purely on a binary `.unity` scene asset which can break across different Unity editor revisions.<br>**Proof:** `SceneBootstrapper.cs` dynamically creates and binds lighting, cameras, probes, volume samplers, and UI canvas on `Awake()`.<br>**Files Affected:** `Assets/Scripts/Bootstrap/SceneBootstrapper.cs`. |
| 4 | 2026-08-27 | Claude & Tsahi | **Analytical Polar Scan-Conversion Display with Geometric Sector Masking.**<br>**Why:** Phase 2 introduces convex ultrasound probe geometry where the acoustic scan lines radiate outward in a fan angle $\theta \in [20^\circ, 120^\circ]$ from a virtual apex of radius $R_c$. Direct Cartesian display grid rasterization with polar boundary testing $(\alpha(x, z), r(x, z))$ generates distortion-free curved 2D ultrasound monitor views with authentic black acoustic masks outside the sector fan, matching clinical machines without requiring intermediate polar interpolation grids.<br>**Alternatives Rejected:** Interpolating raw polar $(N_{\text{lines}} \times N_{\text{samples}})$ texture into a distorted rectangular raw image (rejected because it distorts true anatomical geometry and displays raw acoustic data without physical sector perspective).<br>**Proof:** 10/10 automated tests passed; verified sector boundary angles, acoustic masking outside fan, and 3D sector fan mesh rendering.<br>**Files Affected:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/CoordinateTransform.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Probe/ProbeGeometry.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Probe/ProbeVisualizer.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/CPUSliceGenerator.cs`. |
| 5 | 2026-08-27 | Claude & Tsahi | **Two-Stage Decoupled Polar Acquisition and Cartesian Scan-Conversion Architecture.**<br>**Why:** Clinically and computationally, ultrasound acquisition ($N_{\text{lines}} \times N_{\text{samples}}$ discrete acoustic ray queries) is physically separate from Cartesian video scan conversion ($W_{\text{display}} \times H_{\text{display}}$). Decoupling them via an intermediate zero-allocation `PolarBuffer` ensures that changing probe scan lines or depth samples directly controls 3D volume sampling density and produces observable physical beam discretization (e.g. 16 lines vs 512 lines), while display resolution is independently configurable without redundant 3D volume queries.<br>**Alternatives Rejected:** Direct screen-space backward sampling without a polar buffer (rejected because it bypassed acoustic ray parameters and caused 3D sampling count to be coupled to display resolution).<br>**Proof:** 14/14 unit tests passed; verified exact sample counts ($N_L \times N_S$), zero volume queries in Stage 2, independent display resizing, and bilinear/nearest interpolation.<br>**Files Affected:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/PolarBuffer.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Core/ISliceGenerator.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Probe/ProbeGeometry.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/CPUSliceGenerator.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/SliceRenderer.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/UI/UIController.cs`. |
